NOW Phase 1 — What to buy and do today

Connect Panel 2 (second VT 200S) in parallel to your existing battery — same terminals, (+) to (+) and (−) to (−). This doubles storage to 400Ah at 12V.

Add 1× 200W solar panel on roof. Wire it through a 15A DC fuse to the MPPT controller input.

Wire MPPT controller output through a 20A DC fuse to the battery bank terminals.

Your existing inverter stays exactly as-is — it continues to charge the battery from mains and provide AC backup. No changes needed to it.
FUTURE Phase 2 — Expanding when ready

Add a second 200W panel connected in parallel to the first panel (same + and − bus before the MPPT). Total solar becomes 400W.

The 40A MPPT controller handles up to ~480W at 12V, so no controller upgrade needed.

If you upgrade to a bigger inverter later (e.g. 1500VA), the battery bank already has the capacity to support it. The entire solar side stays the same.

// WIRING SEQUENCE — DO IN THIS ORDER

1
Add second battery in parallel. Connect (+) of Battery 2 to (+) of Battery 1, and (−) to (−). Use thick cable (50mm²), equal lengths. This is your new 400Ah bank.
2
Mount solar panel on roof. Angle 18–22° facing south for Pune. Secure with a tilt mount frame.
3
Connect panel to MPPT controller input (PV+ / PV−) through a 15A DC fuse in the positive line. Use MC4 connectors.
4
Connect MPPT controller output (BAT+ / BAT−) to battery bank through a 20A DC fuse in the positive line. Controller will auto-detect 12V.
5
Your inverter remains connected to the same battery terminals — no change. Both chargers now work in parallel on the same bank without conflict.
6
Phase 2 later: Add second panel in parallel to first (MC4 Y-splitter or separate cable to same MPPT input bus). No other changes needed.
